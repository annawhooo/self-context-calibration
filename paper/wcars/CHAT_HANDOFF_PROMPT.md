# Prompt for the Claude Chat readability pass (paste with the PDF)

I am attaching a compiled conference paper. Read it as a first-time
reader and give me a readability punch list, nothing else.

Rules for the list:

- Every item is one pair: the sentence as written, then the sentence
  as you would write it. Quote the original exactly so I can find it.
- Change wording only. Do not change any number, unit, date, count,
  or the meaning of any claim. Do not add claims, examples,
  hedges, or citations.
- No em dashes anywhere in your suggestions.
- Keep my sentence joins. I join clauses with "and", a colon, or
  "so". Do not split a sentence into short ones, and do not merge
  sentences that are separate on purpose.
- Flag, and do not rewrite, any sentence you had to read twice to
  parse, and say which word you stumbled on.
- Flag any term that is used before the paper defines it, with the
  page where you first met it and the page where it is defined.
- Flag any pronoun ("it", "this", "that", "these") whose referent you
  could not name on first read.
- Do not rewrite the paper, do not summarize it, and do not comment on
  the science. Only the list.

Order the list by page. Cap it at the forty items that matter most.
