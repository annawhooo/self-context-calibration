## The window and how the scripts see it

The freeze is the 2026-09-26 probe day. The committed record ran
past it (2026-09-27 is on main; 2026-09-28 ran while this note was
written), so the paper-side loaders gained one optional cap:
PAPER_DATA_THROUGH=2026-09-26 makes paper/figures/figdata.py drop
verdict lines and daily counts after that date, and tables.py
passes the same date as --end to the null script. Nothing under
probe/ changed. Analysis scripts that take a window were run with
--start 2026-08-02 --end 2026-09-26; the three that do not
(float_census.py, step_change_watch.py, return_watch.py) ran over
the full record, and the 2026-09-27 day they include carries no
breach, no candidacy, and no return (3 CLEAN, 1 ECHO_CHANGE, 1
ERROR), so their freeze figures equal their full-record figures.
