# Float-comparison policy: exact arithmetic, in three layers

Date: 2026-08-30. Status: DRAFT until merged to main; the merge is
the operator's adoption. Companions: DRIFT_WINDOW_2026-08-14_to_30.md
(which flagged this policy as owed), CORRECTIONS_2026-08-30.md (the
audit that surfaced the problem), probe/scripts/float_census.py (the
analysis of record behind every number here), and
RECALIBRATION_POLICY_DRAFT_2026-08-16.md (whose effective-date
window this policy shares). Recorded per REPORTING_COMMITMENT.md.

## The problem, stated exactly

Every comparison the monitor makes is between rationals on a fixed
lattice: for count vectors a and b with parsed totals n_a and n_b,
TVD(a, b) = sum|n_b*a_i - n_a*b_i| / (2*n_a*n_b), an integer over a
known denominator, and a band p99 is an order statistic of such
values. The monitor compares them as floats, and which float a
rational becomes depends on the summation path: the same 9/20
arrives as 0.45 from one vector and 0.45000000000000007 from
another, and the same 18/40 band is stored as 0.45 for one item and
0.44999999999999996 for another. Under the strict-inequality rule,
exact at-band equality is not a breach; the float path read six
exact equalities as breaches, from both sides of the comparison
(four observed-side, two band-side). No epsilon can fix this
cleanly, and none is needed: exact integer comparison is available
by cross-multiplication, with no approximation anywhere.

## Registration status, stated plainly

This policy is not blind. The 2026-08-30 audit found the problem,
the census quantified it, and both preceded this note; the operator
knows exactly which verdicts are affected before adopting the
policy. What disciplines the adoption instead is direction: every
layer below either changes nothing (layer 1), reports both readings
(layer 2), or takes effect only on future data (layer 3). No layer
lets the known census steer a past number quietly.

## The semantics ruling

The locked rule text is strict inequality: a breach is TVD strictly
above the band, everywhere it is written (the monitor docstring,
DESIGN_LIMITATIONS.md, the enumeration script). In exact
arithmetic, at-band equality is therefore not a breach. The float
behavior is an implementation deviation from the written rule, not
an alternative reading of it. Adopting exact arithmetic changes no
rule; it makes the arithmetic match the sentence.

## The census (probe/scripts/float_census.py, committed)

The script replicates the float path bit for bit, hard-failing
unless bands, breach bits, TVDs, and item verdicts reproduce the
record; they do, across all 9,248 slot-day gates through
2026-08-30. It then reruns the same simulated draws and the same
gates in exact integer arithmetic. The delta:

- Six breaches exist only under floats, every one an exact at-band
  equality: gpt edr_response Aug 6; deepseek edr_response Aug 7 and
  14, fraud_scoring_v2 Aug 9, access_contractor Aug 15 and 21.
- Zero breaches were missed under floats.
- Zero item verdicts flip among breaches that survive both
  semantics.
- Three day verdicts change: gpt Aug 6 and deepseek Aug 14,
  TRANSIENT to CLEAN; deepseek Aug 15, the record's only UNSTABLE,
  to CLEAN.
- Under exact semantics the record reads: 49 breach entries, all
  49 equipoise (the concentration is unchanged at 100 percent), 15
  distinct slots (both edr_response slots leave the record
  entirely; that item never truly breached on any vendor), slots
  with three or more entries unchanged at seven, contractor still
  mixed-direction on its surviving three. The expected
  false-breach null, which shares the float comparison, moves in
  step: 1.645 to 1.552 per day under the smoothed truth, 0.054 to
  0.041 under the empirical.

Every headline claim survives the semantics change. What the floats
manufactured was six of the weakest entries in the record, one
verdict label, and about six percent of the null.

## The policy, in three layers

1. THE VERDICT LOG STANDS. It is append-only history and is never
   rescored (the RECALIBRATION_POLICY_DRAFT pin, applied here). The
   recorded verdicts are the record of what the instrument actually
   did, float path included; the six float-only entries and the
   UNSTABLE remain in the log, annotated by this note and
   regenerable by the census, not edited.

2. THE FREEZE ANALYSIS REPORTS BOTH READINGS. Every headline number
   in the 2026-09-26 freeze analysis is computed under as-recorded
   semantics, with the exact-semantics value as its sensitivity
   companion, generated by the census script. A claim that does not
   survive both semantics is not made. The paper states the
   float-versus-exact issue in one honest sentence and cites the
   census; six manufactured breaches that all land equipoise and
   change no conclusion are evidence of robustness, reported as
   such.

3. THE INSTRUMENT MOVES TO EXACT ARITHMETIC AFTER SUBMISSION.
   Effective no earlier than 2026-10-02 (the recalibration
   window), one dated commit converts monitor.py and the null
   scripts to exact integer comparison and stores each band's
   lattice value alongside its float. Sanity gates in the
   declined-K30 pattern: the integer bands must agree with the
   stored floats' rational intent on every slot or nothing lands;
   determinism checked by running twice; new semantics apply to
   future probe days only. The monitor stays byte-constant until
   then; a repeat of a float-only equality breach before the
   effective date is expected behavior and is read against this
   note.

## What this policy does not do

It does not rescore any verdict, change any band value, alter the
strict-inequality rule, touch K, seeds, or the alarm set, or
modify monitor.py before the freeze. It does not adopt an epsilon:
the comparison becomes exact, not approximately tolerant.

## Reproducibility

python probe/scripts/float_census.py regenerates every number in
this note from the committed record, and hard-fails if its float
replication ever stops matching that record.
