# Reporting commitment, declared before monitoring begins

Date: 2026-08-02. Declared before the monitor exists and before any
scheduled observation has run.

Monitoring results will be reported for the full observation window
regardless of whether events occur. A quiet window is a bounded rate
estimate for unannounced behavioral change on pinned model ids, not
an absence of findings, and will be written up with the same care as
an event-bearing window. The observation window, roster, and cadence
in effect are recorded in the verdict log; the write-up reports
whatever they produced.
